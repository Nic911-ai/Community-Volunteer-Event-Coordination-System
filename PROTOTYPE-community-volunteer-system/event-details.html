Community Park Cleanup

📅 Event Information

  • Date and time: Saturday, 15 March 2025, 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM (sample)
  • Location: Riverside Park, main entrance (fictional)

Description

Volunteers work together to clean up a local park so that it is a safe and welcoming space for the community.

✅ Volunteer Responsibilities

  • Collect litter from paths and grass areas.
  • Sort rubbish and recyclables.
  • Follow instructions from the event team.

🎒 Skills or Items That May Be Useful

  • Comfortable outdoor clothing and sturdy shoes.
  • A water bottle.
  • Willingness to work as part of a team.

📝 Registration Details

Volunteers can use the registration form to express interest in this event.

Open questions, to be confirmed with stakeholders: eligibility rules, registration deadline, number of volunteer places, and whether registrations need approval.
